# Web Screen 04: Web Product Detail & Clinical Trial Showcase

## 📌 Overview
The **Web Product Detail Screen** provides an in-depth view of clinical skincare products, including high-resolution image zoom, clinical trial results, ingredient safety breakdowns, and routine pairing suggestions.

---

## 🎨 UI Architecture & Layout Specs

### 1. Left Gallery Column (Width 50%)
- Vertical thumbnail strip + Large main zoom preview image
- High-resolution hover lens zoom for texture inspection
- Video demo tab (application techniques)

### 2. Right Product Info Column (Width 50%)
- Brand badge + Product title + Sub-headline
- Rating stars + link to verified customer reviews
- Variant selector (50ml, 100ml, 200ml)
- Delivery ETA lookup widget with Pincode validator
- Quantity selector + `[ Add to Cart ]` + `[ Buy Now ]` CTAs
- **Beauty Protection Guarantee Box**: Details reaction warranty coverage

### 3. Clinical Data & Ingredients Tabs (Full-Width Bottom)
- **Clinical Results Tab**: Multi-bar breakdown (*94% saw reduced breakouts in 14 days*)
- **Ingredients Safety Audit**: Full INCI list with safety rating tags (Non-comedogenic, Fragrance-free, Paraben-free)
- **How to Apply**: AM/PM routine step sequence
